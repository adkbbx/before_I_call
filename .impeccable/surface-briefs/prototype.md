# Prototype surface
Mode: Operate. One repair-call scenario. Guided demonstration and live voice share the learning controls. Prioritize immediate demo access and recoverable audio failures.

## Direction contract
THESIS: Rehearse the reply that a phrasebook cannot predict. The call screen leads with the current Japanese sentence and has a persistent route to comprehension.
OWN-WORLD: Cool neutral surfaces, ink-blue actions and tinted speech bubbles; Outfit headlines and DM Sans controls. Large readable Japanese with ruby furigana and word meanings on hover, focus or tap.
STORY: Choose a guided demo or set up live practice, hear the building manager, pause and understand, answer, then take away a practice card.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Compact top navigation; a narrow scenario rail and broad conversation workspace; the Japanese reply centered above an audio control strip; explanation opens alongside it on desktop and below on mobile. Signature move: the same sentence stays anchored while its meaning is revealed.
FORM: October 3 user request supersedes the original paper/vermilion prototype. The requested design-taste skill guides the friendly responsive redesign. DESIGN.md and src/styles.css are the active design source.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
